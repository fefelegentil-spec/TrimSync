FAQ

Questions barbers ask

How does TrimSync connect to my Instagram?

TrimSync uses Meta's official Instagram API. You authorize the connection through your Facebook Business account. No password sharing, no third-party access. Setup takes under 5 minutes.

Do I need technical skills to set up TrimSync?

Not at all. If you can post on Instagram, you can set up TrimSync. Our onboarding walks you through every step, and support is always available.

What if the AI doesn't understand a message?

When the AI isn't confident, it flags the message for your review instead of guessing. You get a notification, reply manually if needed, and the AI learns from it.

Can I customize what the bot says?

Absolutely. You set the tone, your services and pricing, your availability, and any specific instructions. The AI speaks in your voice, not a generic robot voice.

Is my clients' data secure?

We only store what's needed for bookings: name and message content. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never sell or share client data. Clients can request deletion at any time at felix@trimsync.tech.
